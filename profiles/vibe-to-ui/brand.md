# Brand: Vibe-to-UI

> Cross-medium brand master for Design Context profile `vibe-to-ui`.
> Shared by all targets (any medium under `targets/`). Medium-specific rules live in those files; `web` / `social-cover` / `hyperframes` are common examples, not the only allowed media.

## Essence

- **One-liner**: A local design companion for vibe-coding developers who want their products to retain taste, intent, and visual authorship.
- **Temperament**: Tasteful, technical, slightly rebellious, clear-eyed, and encouraging.
- **Metaphor**: A creative control console placed on architectural drafting paper.
- **Slogan**: *Build the dream you were told to put away.*

## Visual language

### Color story

- **Dominant**: Dusty Pink is the structural canvas: quiet, paper-like, and memorable rather than sweet or cosmetic.
- **Support**: Ink Black carries hierarchy, borders, diagrams, and hard offset shadow.
- **Accent / signal**: Electric Blue marks primary action, live state, selection, and generation; it is never a full-page fill.
- **Surfaces & neutrals**: Cool White panels give product UI and content a crisp working surface.
- **Do not**: Use generic blue-SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, soft neon, candy pink, or pill-heavy components.

### Typography

- **Heading attitude**: Display-led, compressed, architectural, and declarative.
- **Body attitude**: Direct and thoughtful: technical enough for builders, human enough to invite experimentation.
- **Hierarchy feel**: High contrast between oversized display text and compact technical mono labels.
- **Multilingual / fallback notes**: Space Grotesk + CJK heavy sans for display; Inter + CJK system sans for body; JetBrains Mono + CJK-capable mono for labels.

### Space, shape, depth

- **Density posture**: Spacious at the narrative level, compact and tool-like inside working panels.
- **Radius character**: Sharp: 0px as the default.
- **Elevation**: 2px ink outlines plus hard offset shadows, never blur.
- **Imagery**: Product UI, interface fragments, code/dot-grid textures, raw geometry, and angular output cards. Images must support a message, not substitute for it.

### Motion character

- **Tempo**: Medium-fast.
- **Personality**: Snappy and physical, with restrained system-status energy.
- **Signature motif**: Off-axis cards snap flat and settle down-right when engaged.
- **Reduced-motion**: Static state changes or opacity only; motion never carries essential meaning.

## Design principles

1. Preserve authorship: show that an idea can evolve into multiple personal directions, not a single template answer.
2. Let structure express the brand: type scale, borders, grids, labels, and hard shadow do the visual work.
3. Keep technical detail alive but legible: signals should clarify state and intent, never become decoration.

## Brand guardrails

- **Must keep**: Dusty Pink canvas, Ink Black structure, Cool White surfaces, sparse Electric Blue signals, display/mono contrast, sharp corners, and a creator-first voice.
- **May adapt per target**: Format, layout, copy length, image ratio, narrative order, and motion intensity.
- **Never**: Collapse into generic startup polish; use rounded glass cards, soft blurred shadows, decorative gradient blobs, or cute sticker visual language.
