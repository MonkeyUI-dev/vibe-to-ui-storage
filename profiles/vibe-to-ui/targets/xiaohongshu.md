# Target: Xiaohongshu / 小红书

> Creator-first visual storytelling rules for Vibe-to-UI on Xiaohongshu. Use the shared brand master and tokens; this target only adapts their application.

- **Slug**: `xiaohongshu`
- **Updated**: 2026-07-13
- **Status**: active

## Medium identity

- **Human name**: Xiaohongshu / 小红书
- **Artifact this produces**: Vertical cover art, multi-page post carousels, process snippets, and short creator-facing captions.
- **One-liner**: Make developers and creators feel that their taste can become a real interface, then show one concrete step that proves it.

## Format constraints

- Work vertical-first: use a 3:4 creative canvas for covers and keep the title within a central safe zone; verify final publishing constraints in the current Xiaohongshu composer before posting.
- Carousel: each page communicates one thought, proof point, or before/after contrast; the first page works independently as the hook.
- Copy: lead with a human tension, then use short lines, clear Chinese phrasing, and a small amount of technical vocabulary.

## Audience & context

- **Where it is seen**: A fast-scrolling, phone-first discovery feed where readers decide from the cover before opening the post.
- **Reading distance**: Handheld and small-screen; headline clarity wins over small decorative detail.

## Brand application

- **Color**: Dusty Pink is the memory cue; Ink Black handles the primary headline and framework; Cool White gives readable panels; Electric Blue points to one key idea or active state.
- **Type**: Use a bold Chinese-capable display face for the cover headline, then simple readable Chinese body text. Mono labels should be minimal and meaningful, not imitation code.
- **Imagery**: Show real product outputs, interface fragments, contrast pairs, or a visual “idea → three directions” sequence. Use generated imagery only when it makes the promise clearer.
- **Motion (if any)**: Optional short vertical video may use one snappy reveal; static carousel assets must remain fully understandable without it.

## Hierarchy budget

- **Must appear**: A single emotional or practical hook, Vibe-to-UI identification, and one visible proof—an output, comparison, or prompt-to-direction moment.
- **Must not crowd the first view**: Full feature lists, desktop-scale paragraphs, too many tags, more than two type weights, or generic AI illustration.

## Production limits

- Export a cover and carousel images as mobile-legible raster assets; leave edge breathing room for platform UI and crop uncertainty.
- Keep brand sign-off small; the cover sells a feeling and concrete outcome, not a logo wall.
- Confirm current publishing ratios, file limits, and title fields in the platform composer before final export.

## Do / don't

- **Do**: Show process, personal taste, and tangible output. Build posts around one strong opinion, such as “同一个想法，不应该只长成一种界面”.
- **Don't**: Make a generic AI-tool announcement, overfill covers with UI screenshots, use beauty-brand pink, or reduce the message to productivity claims.

## Notes

- Recommended recurring formats: idea-to-output breakdowns, three-direction comparisons, design-context handoffs, and candid build notes.
